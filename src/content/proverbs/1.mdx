We spend a massive amount of our lives trying to become the smartest person in the room.

We want to figure everything out, secure our own futures, and rely entirely on our own intellect. Slowly, without realizing it, we begin treating ourselves as the highest authority in our lives.

We praise our own hustle.

We trust our own plans.

We elevate our own desires above everything else.

But when we place ourselves at the center of everything, exhaustion follows close behind.

Shortcuts become tempting. Correction feels offensive. Pride disguises itself as independence.

---

## Reverence Changes Perspective

The phrase *"fear of the Lord"* is often misunderstood.

It is not a posture of terror or distrust, as though God is waiting to punish you.

Biblical fear is reverent awe.

It is the grounding realization that you are not the Creator — and you were never meant to carry that burden.

True wisdom begins when surrender replaces self-exaltation.

It is the willingness to look at the vastness of God's authority and willingly submit your life to the One who laid His down for you.

---

## Two Different Postures

| The Illusion of Control | The Posture of Awe |
| :--- | :--- |
| Trusting your intellect as the highest authority | Submitting to God's wisdom above your own |
| Rejecting correction because pride feels threatened | Welcoming instruction because it leads to life |
| Praising self-sufficiency | Finding peace in dependence on God |

---

## The Deeper Truth

Wisdom is not merely about intelligence, strategy, or success.

It is about placement.

When God is restored to His rightful place — exalted, trusted, and revered — you no longer have to carry the crushing weight of pretending to be sovereign over your own life.

Real safety is not found in knowing everything.

It is found in knowing Him.

---

## One Small Step Today

Identify one situation where you are trying to force control through intellect alone. 

Pause. 

Pray before planning. 

Ask God for wisdom before making your next move.

## A Moment to Breathe

*Father, forgive me for the times I have elevated my own plans above Your authority. Teach me to return to a posture of reverence and surrender. Help me trust Your wisdom more than my own understanding. Amen.*
